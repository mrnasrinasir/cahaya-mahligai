Our services

Two ways to bring kompang to your celebration. Both include our own instruments and travel anywhere in Singapore.

About 20–35 min

One-way kompang

Perfect for welcoming the groom's entourage, or adding a special cultural touch to a key moment of your event. Energy and tradition, right where you need it.

About 1–1.5 hrs total

Two-way kompang

The fuller experience: both the arrival welcome and the send-off, creating bookend moments of joy for your day. Ideal for couples who want the complete kompang tradition.

How to book

We recommend booking at least 2–3 months ahead, especially for peak wedding seasons (May–August and November–December).

  1. Get in touch

    Send an enquiry below, or call or WhatsApp us with your date, location and requirements.

  2. Confirm details

    We'll go through timing, venue access, song requests and anything special you'd like included.

  3. Secure your date

    A deposit confirms your booking and reserves our team for your day.

  4. Celebrate together

    We arrive on time, ready to fill your celebration with the joy of kompang.
